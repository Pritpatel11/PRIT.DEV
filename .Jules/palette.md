## 2025-03-01 - Explicit Form Linking in Stylized Components
**Learning:** In highly customized, stylized UI components (like terminal interfaces), users may not immediately recognize labels as interactive elements that focus the corresponding inputs.
**Action:** Always use proper semantic HTML by explicitly linking labels to inputs with `htmlFor` and `id`, and apply utility classes like `cursor-pointer` to labels to visually signal clickability and improve accessibility.

## 2023-10-24 - Semantic Form Accessibility
**Learning:** Forms in styled UI components often omit `id` and `htmlFor` attributes, breaking screen reader association and reducing hit area for clicking. Adding `cursor-pointer` to explicitly linked labels improves perceived clickability.
**Action:** Always explicitly link labels and inputs using `htmlFor`/`id` and add `cursor-pointer` to labels in stylized forms to enhance accessibility and usability.

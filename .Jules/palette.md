## 2024-11-20 - Explicit Form Label Linking
**Learning:** Discovered custom terminal-styled forms lacked explicit label-to-input association.
**Action:** Always link `<label>` and `<input>` elements using `htmlFor` and `id` attributes, and add `cursor-pointer` to stylized labels to visually indicate that clicking them will focus the field.

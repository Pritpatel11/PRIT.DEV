## 2026-08-08 - Link form labels to inputs
**Learning:** Terminal-themed custom form components often forget to link `<label>`s and `<input>`s, harming accessibility for screen readers and reducing click area for sighted users.
**Action:** Ensure all stylised form components explicitly connect labels and inputs with `htmlFor` and `id` attributes, and use `cursor-pointer` on labels to signal clickability visually.

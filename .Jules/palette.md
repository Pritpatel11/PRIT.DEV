## 2025-02-18 - Missing Ad-Hoc Close Button ARIA Labels and Unlinked Form Labels
**Learning:** Ad-hoc close buttons (like alerts/toasts) often lack aria-labels, and custom stylized forms frequently omit explicit id/htmlFor linking for screen readers, reducing accessibility.
**Action:** Always add aria-labels to icon-only close buttons and explicitly link labels to inputs with id and htmlFor.

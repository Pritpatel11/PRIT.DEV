## 2024-10-13 - Explicit Form Label Linking
**Learning:** Users with motor impairments or those using touch devices struggle to focus small input fields. Explicitly linking labels using `htmlFor` and `id` expands the clickable area to include the label text, significantly improving usability while maintaining screen reader compatibility.
**Action:** Always link form labels to their inputs and apply `cursor-pointer` to stylized labels to visually communicate this expanded click target to users.
